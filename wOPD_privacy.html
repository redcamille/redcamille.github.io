Privacy Policy for wOPD

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Overview

wOPD is a Windows desktop application for retrieving, processing, organizing, and managing patent documents.

This Privacy Policy explains how wOPD handles Google user data when using its optional Google Drive upload feature.

2. Google Drive Access

wOPD uses Google OAuth 2.0 and requests the following permission:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission allows wOPD to create files in Google Drive and access files created or opened through the application.

wOPD does not request unrestricted access to the user’s Google Drive.

3. Use of Google User Data

Google Drive authorization is used solely to upload files selected or generated through wOPD and to perform operations necessary to complete those uploads.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

wOPD does not sell or share Google user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes. Google account authorization data is processed only as necessary to provide the Google Drive upload functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

wOPD processes patent documents locally on the user’s computer.

Google OAuth credentials are used to authorize Google Drive uploads.

5. Data Sharing

wOPD transfers user-selected files to Google Drive through Google’s authorized APIs.

Google user data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising.

6. Access Revocation and Data Deletion

Users can revoke wOPD’s Google Drive access through their Google Account settings: https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Files previously uploaded to Google Drive remain under the user’s control and can be managed or deleted through Google Drive.

Revoking access does not automatically delete previously uploaded files.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

wOPD’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.
